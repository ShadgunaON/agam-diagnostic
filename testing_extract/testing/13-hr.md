# 13 — HR: Leave, Attendance, Probation, Holidays

> Status legend: ✅ Pass · ❌ Fail · ⚠️ Blocked · ➖ N/A · ⬜ Not tested. See [README](README.md).

The HR/Time module: leave requests & approvals, attendance (auto-fill + corrections), holidays, probation workflow, and HR reports. Leave/absence here feeds **payroll LOP** (see [12 — Payroll](12-payroll.md)). Governed by `hr.leaves.*`, `hr.attendance.*`, employee self-service perms.

> A deeper HR QA doc exists — also run **[docs/HR-MODULE-QA.md](../HR-MODULE-QA.md)**.

## A. Scope & routes

- `/app/hr` — HR overview.
- `/app/hr/attendance`, `/app/hr/my-time` — attendance.
- `/app/hr/leave`, `/app/hr/leave-calendar`, `/app/leaves` (+ `/new`, `/[id]`) — leave.
- `/app/hr/holidays` — holidays.
- `/app/hr/probation` — probation.
- `/app/hr/reports` — reports.
- `/app/attendance` (+ `/[id]`, `/correction-requests`, `/my-corrections`) — attendance records & corrections.

## B. Preconditions

- Employees exist (A3 as a regular employee; a manager/approver for approvals).
- Holiday calendar and leave types configured.
